Anwar Masood at DIGS — Annual Prize Distribution 2017

A notable literary and cultural moment in the institution’s history.

A distinguished literary guest at the annual ceremony.

On 26 February 2017, renowned Punjabi poet Anwar Masood participated as Chief Guest at the Annual Prize Distribution Ceremony of Danish International Grammar School & Girls College.

The visit reflects DIGS’s effort to expose students and families to literary, cultural and intellectual personalities beyond the routine school environment.
