DIGS Annual Day — 2017

A major school-community gathering bringing together parents, educators, guests and institutional representatives.

Annual celebration and community engagement.

DIGS Annual Day 2017 was held at Madina Marriage Hall, Karianwala.

Ch. Muhammad Farooq, Deputy Director Education, Gujrat, attended as Chief Guest. Abid Iqbal Khari, President of Positive Pakistan, was among the distinguished guests.

The event included speeches, formal guest recognition and strong parent/community attendance.
