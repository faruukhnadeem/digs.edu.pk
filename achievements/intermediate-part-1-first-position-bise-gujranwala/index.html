Intermediate Part-I First Position — BISE Gujranwala

A major academic achievement for Danish International Girls College under the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Gujranwala.

Academic performance formally recognized.

Danish International Girls College secured first position in Intermediate Part-I under BISE Gujranwala.

In the formal recognition photographs, the student receives the prize from then Chairman BISE Gujranwala, Ch. Muhammad Aslam Sikho, at the Chairman’s office. Hafiz Muhammad Faruukh Nadeem is present with the student and school representatives.

The achievement was later celebrated at DIGS with staff, community representatives and institutional leadership.
