Official Visit by the Chairman of BISE Gujranwala — 2014

An early official educational visit during the institution’s formative years in Dhudray Sharqi.

Early engagement with senior educational leadership.

In 2014, Ch. Muhammad Aslam Sikho, then Chairman of the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Gujranwala, visited Danish International Grammar School.

The visit included a campus walkthrough and interaction with school representatives. The Chairman appreciated multiple aspects of the developing institution, including its building, learning environment, staff organization, teaching methodology and campus development.

Faruukh Nadeem received and managed the visit in his educational-leadership role during the school’s early years.
