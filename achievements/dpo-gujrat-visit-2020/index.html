DPO Gujrat Visit — 8 October 2020

A meaningful public-engagement moment in the institution’s continuing journey.

A notable visit to the school community.

On 8 October 2020, Muhammad Umar Farooq Salamat, then District Police Officer (DPO), Gujrat, visited Danish International Grammar School & Girls College in Dhudray Sharqi.

During the program, he addressed the school community, interacted with institutional representatives and participated in student-recognition activities.

In his recorded remarks, he referred to inquiries he had made about the institution before visiting and indicated that what he observed was consistent with the positive information he had already received about the educational work being carried out in the area.
