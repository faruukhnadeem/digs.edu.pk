Institutional Partnership · 17 April 2025

UMT–DIGS Memorandum of Understanding

A formal partnership connecting school education with higher-education opportunities and professional development.

Connecting institutions for wider student and staff opportunity.

On 17 April 2025, Danish International Grammar School entered into a formal Memorandum of Understanding with the University of Management and Technology.

The collaboration supports areas including internships and employment opportunities, workshops and seminars, capability enhancement, guest-speaker sessions, specialized skills-development workshops, courses and certifications.

Areas of Collaboration
  • Internships and employment opportunities
  • Workshops and seminars
  • Capability enhancement
  • Guest-speaker sessions
  • Specialized skills development
  • Courses and certifications

Partnership as part of institutional growth.

The MoU reflects DIGS’s commitment to connecting school and college education with wider learning and professional opportunities.
