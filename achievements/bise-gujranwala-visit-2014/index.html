Institutional Milestone · 2014

Official Visit by the Chairman of BISE Gujranwala

An early milestone in the development of Danish International Grammar School during its formative years in Dhudray Sharqi.

Early engagement with senior educational leadership.

In 2014, Ch. Muhammad Aslam Sikho, then Chairman of the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Gujranwala, visited Danish International Grammar School in Dhudray Sharqi.

The visit included a walkthrough of the developing campus and interaction with school representatives. The Chairman expressed appreciation for multiple aspects of the institution, including the school environment, staff organization, teaching approach and overall development.

The visit came soon after classes had commenced and became an important early milestone in the institution’s continuing educational journey.
