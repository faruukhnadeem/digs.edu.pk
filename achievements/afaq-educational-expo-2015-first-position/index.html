First Position at AFAQ Educational Expo — 2015

DIGS students represented the institution at a district-level educational platform combining participation, public speaking and recognition.

District-level participation and recognition.

In 2015, Danish International Grammar School participated in the AFAQ Educational Expo in Gujrat, where schools from across District Gujrat took part.

DIGS secured first position at the event. The available photographs show students participating on stage, receiving recognition shields and representing the school at the expo.

The exact competition category is not stated in the surviving material, so the website records the achievement without inventing a category.
