Our Story

An institution built around community need and educational opportunity.

The journey from construction in 2011 to regular classes in 2013 laid the foundation for a school and girls college serving Dhudray Sharqi and surrounding communities.

Institutional Journey

From an educational idea to a growing institution.

The institution was developed in response to limited access to suitable educational opportunities within the local rural area.

Construction began in 2011. Classes commenced in 2013, supported by local families and community trust. Over time, the initiative developed into school and girls college pathways, student-development activities, official educational engagement and institutional partnerships.

Purpose

Vision, mission and values.

Vision

To prepare students for meaningful futures through academic learning, character, confidence, responsibility and a positive connection with community.

Mission

To provide accessible, structured education that supports academic excellence, personal development and lifelong learning.

Values

Learning, integrity, discipline, respect, responsibility, opportunity and service.

Leadership

Institutional leadership grounded in practical school management.

DIGS is led through academic, administrative and campus-management responsibilities focused on institutional continuity and student learning.
