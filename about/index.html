About DIGS

An institution built around educational opportunity.

From construction beginning in 2011 to regular classes in 2013 and continued institutional growth, DIGS has developed through education, community trust, student opportunity and partnerships.

Institutional Journey

From an idea in a rural community to a growing school and girls college.

DIGS was developed to expand access to structured educational opportunity for families in Dhudray Sharqi and surrounding communities.

The school’s story includes academic development, student competitions, official educational visits, annual functions, parent education, notable visitors and institutional partnerships.

Vision, Mission & Values

Education that develops knowledge, character and confidence.

Vision

To prepare students for meaningful futures through learning, character, confidence, responsibility and opportunity.

Mission

To provide accessible, structured education that supports academic progress, personal growth and community connection.

Values

Learning, integrity, discipline, respect, responsibility, faith, opportunity and service.

Selected Timeline

Milestones in a continuing journey.

2011 — Institution-building begins

Development of the educational institution begins in Dhudray Sharqi.

2013 — Regular classes commence

The school begins its formal teaching journey.

2014 — Chairman BISE Gujranwala visits

An early official educational visit recognizes the institution’s developing environment and systems.

2015 — Student achievement and talent development

DIGS secures first position at the AFAQ Educational Expo and hosts the Little Champs Award program.

2017–2018 — Annual functions and notable guests

Literary, educational and community engagement expands through annual functions and guest participation.

2020 — DPO Gujrat visit

A notable public-engagement milestone involving students, staff and school representatives.

2023–2025 — Parenting education

Recurring parent-engagement sessions strengthen school-family partnership.

2025 — UMT–DIGS MoU

A formal institutional partnership is signed with the University of Management and Technology.

Leadership

Hafiz Muhammad Faruukh Nadeem, professionally known as Faruukh Nadeem, serves as Chief Administrator and has been associated with the institution’s development, school management, partnerships and long-term growth.
