Parenting Education & School–Family Partnership

Recurring parent education reflects DIGS’s effort to connect school culture, family understanding and student development.

A recurring institutional parent-engagement initiative.

DIGS conducted parenting and parent-engagement sessions in 2023, 2024 and 2025.

One recurring theme was “Understanding School Culture”, helping parents better understand school expectations, educational partnership and the shared role of families and educators in children’s development.

Chief Administrator Faruukh Nadeem led sessions with large parent participation.
