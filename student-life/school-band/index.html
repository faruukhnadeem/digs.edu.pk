Danashians School Band

A recurring co-curricular activity that combines teamwork, ceremonial responsibility and school spirit.

Discipline expressed through coordinated participation.

The Danashians School Band provides students with opportunities to develop confidence, timing, teamwork, responsibility and ceremonial discipline.

The band participates in school functions and formal gatherings, representing institutional identity through coordinated uniforms, percussion and flag ceremonies.
