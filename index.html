About DIGS

Built to expand educational opportunity in a rural community.

The institution-building journey began in Dhudray Sharqi in 2011. Regular classes commenced in 2013, developing over time into school and girls college pathways serving local families and surrounding communities.

The school’s development has been shaped by academic ambition, community trust, student support, teacher development, extracurricular opportunity and institutional partnerships.
