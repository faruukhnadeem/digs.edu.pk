UMT–DIGS Memorandum of Understanding — 17 April 2025

A formal collaboration connecting DIGS with higher-education opportunities, professional development and wider institutional engagement.

Building institutional connections beyond the school campus.

On 17 April 2025, Danish International Grammar School formalized a Memorandum of Understanding with the University of Management and Technology (UMT).

The collaboration supports areas including internships and employment opportunities, workshops and seminars, capability enhancement, guest-speaker sessions, specialized skills development, courses and certifications.

Chief Administrator Faruukh Nadeem represented DIGS during the formal signing and institutional discussions.
