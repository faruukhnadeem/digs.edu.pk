Academics

Progressive learning pathways from early years to intermediate education.

Academic programs are structured around age-appropriate learning, subject development, board preparation and student progression.

Early Years

Preschool

Foundational language, numeracy, classroom routines, creativity and social development in a supportive early-learning environment.

Primary

Primary School

Core subject foundations, reading, writing, mathematics, science awareness, communication and positive learning habits.

Middle

Middle School

Progressive subject knowledge, academic responsibility, assessment and preparation for secondary-level learning.

Secondary

Matric

Focused secondary education and board preparation with subject guidance, assessment and academic monitoring.

Girls College

Intermediate Programs

Intermediate pathways including ICS, F.A and other programs offered according to the current admission cycle and institutional approvals.

Islamic Learning

Quran Education

Nazra Quran and Hifz-e-Quran opportunities form part of the institution’s wider educational environment.

Learning Approach

Academic development supported by school culture.

Our approach combines classroom learning with discipline, student recognition, co-curricular participation, parent engagement and regular institutional activities.

Admission Information

Danish International Grammar School & Girls College serves students and families from Dhudray Sharqi and surrounding communities through school education, intermediate pathways, student development and community engagement.
